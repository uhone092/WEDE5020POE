About MTVI Apparel

MTVI Apparel is a clothing brand that focuses on producing stylish, high-quality clothing at an affordable price.

The brand was created to give people an opportunity to express themselves through fashion while celebrating modern African culture.

Our History

MTVI Apparel produces sustainably sourced, high-quality urban fashion and essentials. What started as limited-run pop-up markets has grown into a recognized local lifestyle brand operating two flagship retail spaces.

Our Mission

Committed to designing and delivering premium quality clothing at an affordable price.

Our Vision

To become the leading clothing brand that represents Venda culture.
